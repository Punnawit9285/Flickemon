## How the project keeps its use as narrow as it can

These are deliberate constraints, not incidental facts. Changing any of them
changes the project's exposure, so treat each as a rule rather than a habit:

- **Non-commercial.** Nothing here is sold. No licensing, no advertising, no
  paid tiers, no in-app purchases, no cryptocurrency or token of any kind, and
  no charge of any kind for the extension or anything inside it.

  Voluntary donations toward running costs are accepted, and they buy nothing.
  That boundary is the whole of the rule: a donation must never unlock a
  feature, a Pokémon, a shop item, a boost, or early access to any of them,
  because a donation that buys something is a paid tier under a friendlier
  name. Every student gets the same game whether they have given money or not.

  The Poké Mart is not an exception and must not become one. Its prices are in
  Poké Dollars, which are earned only by watching lectures — there is
  deliberately no way to acquire them faster than by studying, and adding one
  would turn an in-game sink into a storefront.
- **Educational, and internal.** Flickémon exists to make lecture-watching on
  Flick less of a slog for students at Chulalongkorn's Faculty of Medicine. It
  is distributed within that organisation, not to the general public.
- **No public store listing.** The extension is not published on the Chrome Web
  Store or any other public marketplace. It is installed unpacked, or from a zip
  shared internally. A store listing is a materially more visible use and would
  need its own decision — see [PROVENANCE](sprites/PROVENANCE.md#rights).
- **No game files.** Nothing here is extracted from a Pokémon game, ROM, or ROM
  dump. The sprites come from a public community dataset; no game code, audio,
  music, or text dump is used.
- **No official branding.** The extension does not use the Pokémon logo, the
  Poké Ball mark, official box or promotional art, or any Nintendo trademark in
  its own icon, name styling, or listing imagery.
- **No substitute for the real thing.** Flickémon is a study timer with a battle
  minigame attached. It does not reproduce a Pokémon game, does not compete with
  one, and gives nobody a reason to buy one less.

## What this notice does not do

Worth being straight about, because a disclaimer file can create a false sense
of safety:

- A disclaimer is **not a licence**. Saying "we don't own this" correctly
  describes the situation; it does not create permission to use the material.
  Fan projects like this one exist at the rights holders' discretion.
- Nintendo has historically acted against fan projects, including non-commercial
  ones, and has not treated "free" or "educational" as a defence.
- Non-commercial educational use is a favourable fact under most fair-use and
  fair-dealing analyses, and under Thai copyright law's research and teaching
  exceptions — but it is one factor, not a safe harbour, and wholesale copying of
  2,050 pieces of artwork weighs the other way.
- The realistic worst case for a project in this posture is a takedown request,
  which the section above is designed to make cheap to honour. Keeping the
  constraints above intact is what keeps it there.
