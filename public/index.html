Flickémon

An unofficial study companion for Flick. Not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures Inc., GAME FREAK inc. or The Pokémon Company.

Study your lectures. Raise a companion.

Flickémon is a free, unofficial browser extension for students using Flick, the lecture platform of the Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University. It turns the time you already spend watching lectures into a game: your companion earns experience while a lecture plays, and evolves as the term goes on.

What it does

  • Earn as you watch. Active playback on a lecture page awards experience and in-game currency.
  • Catch and evolve. Build a party from over a thousand species, shinies included.
  • Battle and trade. Challenge a classmate with a six-digit code, or trade companions.
  • Study together. An opt-in leaderboard and a friends list, both of which you control.
  • Sync across devices. Sign in with your faculty Google account and your progress follows you.

Your data

Flickémon reads the playback state of the lecture video, and nothing else. It does not read lecture contents, exam questions, transcripts or keystrokes, and it runs on flick.docchula.com only — no other site.

There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no sale of data of any kind. Signing in is optional; without it, everything stays on your device.

The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Signing in

Cloud sync uses Google Sign-In, and asks only for your name, email address and profile picture. It is used to identify your save and nothing else. On a shared computer — a library machine, say — Flickémon will only sign in as the account already signed in to Flick, so your study time cannot be credited to somebody else by accident.
